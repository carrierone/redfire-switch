Legal Notice - ECPA Compliance

Electronic Communications Privacy Act (ECPA) Compliance

This system operates under the provider exception specified in 18 U.S.C. § 2511(2)(a)(i) for fraud prevention and network protection.

Authorized Use Only

  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • All activities are logged and monitored
  • Unauthorized access is prohibited and may result in criminal prosecution
  • Data must be used solely for fraud detection and prevention purposes

Data Protection Requirements

  • Recordings in memory storage are for real-time fraud detection only
  • Disk storage requires legal authorization or high-confidence fraud indicators
  • Data minimization principles must be followed
  • Retention periods must comply with established policies

Incident Reporting

Any suspected compliance violations, unauthorized access attempts, or security incidents must be immediately reported to the compliance team.
